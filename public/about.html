About Glorious Medical Centre

A proudly Papua New Guinean-owned facility dedicated to affordable, quality healthcare

A New Standard of Care in Port Moresby

Glorious Medical Centre (GMC) is a newly established, proudly Papua New Guinean-owned health facility located in Badili, Port Moresby. Our primary goal is simple — to make quality healthcare affordable and accessible for every Papua New Guinean and international resident in our nation.

We are committed to providing thorough, competent, and compassionate medical services — backed by highly experienced healthcare professionals, modern facilities, and up-to-date medical equipment. Whether you need a general consultation, specialist referral, or emergency care, GMC is here for you.
